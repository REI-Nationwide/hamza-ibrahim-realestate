Information we collect

If you use the contact form, we collect the information you choose to provide. That may include your name, email address, a phone number if you enter one, your message, and whether you checked the optional box agreeing to be contacted by eXp Realty Holdings via call, email, and text for real estate services.

We use the information you submit to respond to your inquiry and to provide real estate services you request. If you opt in, we may contact you by call, email, and text for real estate services, including to confirm appointments, send reminders, and notify you of schedule updates or important changes.

Sharing and sale of data

This section applies in all states, including states other than California.

Data will not be sold or shared with third parties for promotional or marketing purposes.

We do not sell phone numbers. SMS opt-in data and consent are not shared with third parties.

We may use a form-processing service to deliver your message to hamza.ibrahim@expcommercial.com. That service acts only as a processor so the inquiry can be received. It is not a sale or sharing of data for promotional or marketing purposes.

Text messaging (SMS)

If you agree to text messages, the following terms apply:

  • We use SMS to confirm appointments, send reminders, and notify clients of schedule updates or important changes related to real estate services.
  • You can cancel the SMS service at any time. Just text “STOP”. After you send the SMS message “STOP” to us, we will send you an SMS message to confirm that you have been unsubscribed. After this, you will no longer receive SMS messages from us. If you want to join again, just sign up as you did the first time, and we will start sending SMS messages to you again.

How to contact us
